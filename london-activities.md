- **[Battersea Park in Concert](https://www.batterseaparkinconcert.com/movies-and-musicals)** — Movies & Musicals + Royal Philharmonic (Aug Bank Holiday)
- **[Open Garden Squares Weekend](https://www.opensquares.org/)** (June)
- **[Canalway Cavalcade](https://waterways.org.uk/support/ways-to-get-involved/events/iwa-canalway-cavalcade-2026)** — Little Venice (May Bank Holiday)
- **[Philippines Summer Festival](https://empireevents.uk/events/philippines-summer-festival-2026)** — New River Sports Centre (July)
- **[Summer Sounds (King's Cross)](https://www.kingscross.co.uk/event/summer-sounds-music-festival)** — free open-air music festival at Coal Drops Yard: folk, jazz, soul, gospel, samba, opera and beatboxing, drop-in (Thu 13 – Sun 23 Aug)
- **[Art Car Boot Fair (King's Cross)](https://www.kingscross.co.uk/event/art-car-boot-fair)** — 120+ artists selling out of car boots, Lewis Cubitt Square, ticketed (Sat 5 Sep)
- **[Classic Car Boot Sale (King's Cross)](https://www.kingscross.co.uk/event/classic-car-boot-sale)** — vintage fashion, 70 classic cars and a Routemaster bar across Granary Square & Coal Drops Yard, £8 (Sat 26 – Sun 27 Sep)
- **[Electric Summer (Battersea Power Station)](https://batterseapowerstation.co.uk/events/electric-summer-presented-by-geely-auto-uk/)** — riverside funfair: big wheel, dodgems, wave swinger and a new roller rink (Wed 29 Jul – Mon 31 Aug)
- **[celebrASIA (Battersea Power Station)](https://batterseapowerstation.co.uk/celebrasia/)** — three days of South East Asian street food, performances and an artisan market in the Turbine Halls; mostly free (Fri 11 – Sun 13 Sep)
- **[Magic of Thailand](https://magicofthailand.co.uk/)** — Thai food, culture & concerts, Ealing Common (Sat 5 – Sun 6 Sep)
- **[Japan Matsuri](https://japanmatsuri.com/)** — the UK's biggest festival of Japanese culture and food: street food stalls, stage performances, martial arts demos and the communal IBUKI singalong. Free, Trafalgar Square, one-in-one-out once full (Sun 4 Oct, 10am–8pm)
- **[Cocktails in the City](https://cocktailsinthecity.com/)** — pop-up bars from 20 of London's best in a private Georgian garden (Jul 2–4 & Aug 6–8)
- **[EFG London Jazz Festival](https://efglondonjazzfestival.org.uk/)** — ten days of jazz across the city, from the Barbican and Southbank Centre to small clubs (Fri 13 – Sun 22 Nov)
- **[Pitchfork Music Festival London](https://pitchforkmusicfestival.co.uk/)** — a week of curated gigs at KOKO, Roundhouse, EartH, Hackney Church, Village Underground and more, rather than one big site (Mon 2 – Sun 8 Nov)
