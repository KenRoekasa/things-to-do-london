# 🇬🇧 London Guide: Curated Activities

## 🎯 Competitive Socialising, Games & Sports

- **Football/Games:** Toca Social
- **Table Tennis:** Bounce (Old Street)
- **Mini Golf:** Puttshack, Topgolf, Crazy Boules, Plonk
- **Arcade/Retro Gaming:** Four Quarters (Southwark Quarter), NQ64, Four Thieves (Battersea), Sky High Retrotainment, Bloodsports, Arcade Bars
- **Darts:** Flight Club
- **Pool:** Poolhouse UK
- **Battle & Tech Games:** Hijingo, Boom Battle Bar (The O2), Fairgame (Canary Wharf), Battlekarts (St Albans), Krazy Karts, Activate, Quiz Boxing, Live Game Show Experience, Immersive Gamebox
- **Board Games:** Draughts Board Game, Gameroom
- **Escape Rooms/Immersive:** Escape Room, Enigma Quests, Sensas, Phantom Peaks, Crystal Maze, Escape Room Arcade, Monopoly Lifesized, Mission: Breakout London
- **Bowling & Active:** Lane 7, Queens Skate and Dine, Ninja Warrior
- **Other Play:** Roxy Ball Room, Dabbers Social Bingo, The Thieves
- **Shooting:** Clays Bar
- **Theme Park:** Babylon

## 🎭 Entertainment & Immersive Experiences

- **Theatre/Shows:** Theatre, Musical Con, Paddington Musical, The Lion King, Witness for the Prosecution (County Hall)
- **Cinema:** Electric Cinema, Backyard Cinema, Open Air Cinema Kings Cross, Rooftop Film Club
- **Unique/Immersive:** Circe Waterloo, Jurassic World, The Murder Express (Bethnal Green), Paradox Museum, Frameless Immersive Experience, Bubble Planet, Hexmoor: Wizarding Prison, Moco Museum
- **Music/Events:** Candlelight Concerts, Live Band Karaoke, Lucky Voice (Karaoke), West End Musical Brunch, Studio 338 (outdoor club / garden parties)
- **Immersive Cocktails:** Avora Experience, Moonshine Saloon

## 🥂 Bars, Rooftops & Dining

- **Social Hubs:** Flat Iron Square (Southwark — weekly Speedquizzing / Club Del Marg / Seshions / Matchday Social + World Cup 2026 fan hub at The Lowline), Rae's (arches bar + pizza)
- **Rooftop Bars:** Sky Gardens (also sunrise yoga), 1864 Rooftop Bar, Aviary Rooftop Restaurant & Terrace Bar, London Bridge Rooftop
- **Unique Dining/Drinks:** Cahoots Underground, Cahoots Postal Office (1940s speakeasy), Chocolate Cocktail Club, Inamo (Covent Garden), The Bermondsey Bierkeller, The Escapologist, Melt Chocolates, Bife Steak, Vinegar Yard, Korean Buldak cocktail bar (fried chicken)
- **Themed Pubs & Bars:** Flower Pot, Pretty Decent Victoria Park Taproom, Venn St. Records, Waxy O'Connor's (Fantasy Pub), LOTR-themed pub (central London)
- **Alternative:** Chicken Rush

## 🍗 Food To Try

- **Chicken Wings:** Wingman, Fowl, Chick and Beer, Inyun
- **American/Other:** Passyunk Avenue, Smoking Goat

## 🛍️ Markets & Shopping

- **Markets:** South Bank Market, Spitalfields Market, Brick Lane, Seven Dials Market, Camden Market, Coal Drops Yard (Kings Cross), Japanese Street Market
- **Shopping:** Shop Off Piste (Kings Cross)

## 🏞️ Outdoors & General Activities

- **General Outdoors:** Southbank, Trafalgar Square Musical Events, Skuna Boats (canal trip), Secret Garden South Bank, Pedal Boats Hyde Park (Serpentine), Primrose Hill (sunrise / skyline picnic), Thames Clipper (golden hour)
- **Summer/Nature:** Lavender Field, Strawberry Picking, Peony Farm, [The UK's No.1 Tulip Festival](https://share.google/sWIvIJnN2jeEdRptb)
- **Arts & Crafts:** Kcraft Studio
- **Comedy:** Secret Comedy Club

## 🎪 Summer & Annual Festivals

- **West End Live** — free musical theatre, Leicester Square (June)
- **Taste of London** — Regent's Park food festival (June)
- **London's Biggest Pizza Festival** — Slice-o-Mania (summer)
- **Hampton Court Palace Festival** (June)
- **Battersea Park in Concert** — Movies & Musicals + Royal Philharmonic (Aug Bank Holiday)
- **Open Garden Squares Weekend** (June)
- **Canalway Cavalcade** — Little Venice (May Bank Holiday)
- **Philippines Summer Festival** — New River Sports Centre (July)
